Somewhere between dreams and death, they found each other.

Two Lives

They loved each other in a world neither could explain.

Written by Loveline

Chapter 1: The Dream Girl

Every night, Elias dreamed of a girl standing beneath a tree covered in silver flowers.

She wore a blue dress and called him by a name he had never heard.

"You came back," she would say.

He always woke before he could ask what she meant.

One afternoon, he visited a museum and froze before an old painting. It showed the same girl, wearing the same blue dress. The plaque said the portrait was over a hundred years old.

Her name was Mira Vale. She had died in 1924.

Chapter 2: The Other Side

That night, Elias returned to the dream. This time, the girl was crying.

"You found my picture," she said.

"Who are you?"

"Someone who has been waiting."

She told him that she lived in a place between worlds. Once, she had loved a man who promised to return after a war. He never came back, and Mira died believing she had been forgotten.

"I am not that man," Elias insisted.

Mira touched his cheek.

"Not yet."

Chapter 3: The Hidden Door

Elias discovered an abandoned chapel near the museum. Beneath its floor was a door covered in symbols matching those in Mira's painting.

When he touched the lock, the world changed. Silver flowers grew through the stone, and a cold wind carried a voice from the other side.

He opened the door and saw Mira standing beneath her tree.

For one impossible hour, they walked together. She told him about her life; he told her about his. They laughed, argued, and discovered that they loved the same little things.

Then the sky began to crack.

"You cannot stay," Mira whispered. "The door takes something from both worlds every time it opens."

Chapter 4: The Last Dream

Elias returned to the chapel with a plan. He would destroy the door so neither world could keep stealing from the other.

Mira begged him to stop.

"If you close it, you may never see me again."

"And if I leave it open, other people could be lost."

They stood together beneath the silver tree. For the first time, Elias understood why Mira had waited so long: she had mistaken longing for a promise that the world owed her.

"I love you," he said. "But love cannot mean keeping you trapped."

He closed the door.

Years passed. Elias lived, loved, and grew old. He never forgot the girl in the blue dress.

On the last night of his life, he dreamed of silver flowers one final time. Mira waited beneath the tree, no longer crying.

"You came back," she whispered.

Elias smiled.

"I told you I would."

And when morning came, the painting in the museum had changed. Beside Mira stood a man with kind eyes and silver at his temples.

No one knew who he was.
