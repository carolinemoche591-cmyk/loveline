The Heir

The weakest boy in the kingdom carried its most dangerous secret.

Written by Loveline

Chapter 1: The Servant's Son

Everyone in the kingdom of Aurelion knew that Prince Darius had no magic. At seventeen, he could not summon a spark, lift a stone with his mind, or heal a paper cut.

His half-brothers mocked him. The court ignored him. Even his father called him a disappointment.

Then, during the royal ceremony, an assassin attacked the king.

Darius moved before anyone else. A wall of blue fire rose from his hands, throwing the assassin across the hall.

The entire court fell silent.

The king stared at him in terror.

"Impossible," he whispered. "We destroyed that bloodline."

Chapter 2: The Secret Mother

Darius was taken to a hidden chamber beneath the palace. There, the king confessed that Darius's mother had been a guardian of the ancient star magic, a power feared because it could break any spell—including the spell that kept the royal family immortal.

"You are not my son," the king said. "You are a weapon someone placed in my house."

"Then why did you keep me?"

"Because I believed I could control you."

Darius fled the palace that night. In the forest, a mysterious warrior named Sera found him.

"If you want to survive," she said, "stop trying to prove you belong to people who fear you."

Chapter 3: The Betrayal

Sera trained Darius to control his magic. He learned that his power was connected to the stars, and each spell carried a cost: a memory, a feeling, or a piece of his strength.

They became close. For the first time, Darius trusted someone without needing to earn it.

Then Sera disappeared with the map to his mother's hidden sanctuary.

He found her at the palace, standing beside his eldest brother.

"I was promised freedom," she said, unable to meet his eyes.

Darius felt the betrayal like a blade. But when his brother ordered her execution, Darius stepped between them.

"She betrayed me," he said. "That doesn't make her yours to kill."

Chapter 4: The Throne

The king's immortality spell was failing. Without it, the royal family would lose its power—and the king intended to sacrifice Darius to renew it.

Darius returned to the palace with Sera at his side. His brothers attacked, but he refused to destroy them.

Instead, he broke the spell.

The palace shook. The stars shone through the ceiling, and every stolen year returned to the people the king had harmed.

The king aged in a single breath, falling to his knees.

Darius was offered the throne. He accepted only after the council agreed that no ruler would ever again hold absolute power.

Later, Sera apologized. Darius forgave her, but trust took longer.

"You don't have to make me your enemy," she said.

"And you don't get to decide that you're my friend just because you're sorry," he answered.

She nodded. It was a beginning, not a happy ending yet.

For the first time, Darius ruled a kingdom where people could choose who they became.
