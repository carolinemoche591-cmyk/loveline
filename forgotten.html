What happens when the heart remembers what the mind forgets?

Forgotten

She forgot the man who loved her. She remembered the man who lied.

Written by Loveline

Chapter 1: The Accident

When Lena woke in the hospital, a stranger was holding her hand.

He had tired eyes and a small scar near his eyebrow. He looked as though he had been crying for days.

"Lena," he whispered. "You're awake."

"Who are you?"

The question broke something in his face.

"I'm Noah. I'm your boyfriend."

The doctors explained that the accident had affected her memory. Lena remembered her family, her school, and her childhood, but the last two years were a blank page.

Noah promised to help her remember. He did not tell her that they had planned to get married the following month.

Chapter 2: The Other Man

During rehabilitation, Lena received visits from a man named Adrian. He brought flowers, made her laugh, and seemed to understand her better than Noah did.

"We used to be close," Adrian explained.

Noah disliked him immediately, though he could not explain why without sounding jealous.

One afternoon, Lena found an old photograph of herself and Adrian kissing.

When she asked Noah about it, he looked away.

"You were confused back then."

"Did I love him?"

"You loved me."

It was not an answer, and Lena knew it.

Chapter 3: The Missing Years

Lena began searching through her old messages. She found arguments, apologies, and a series of messages from Noah that frightened her.

Please don't tell her what happened.

She can't know about the night at the bridge.

She confronted Adrian. At first, he refused to speak. Then he confessed that he and Lena had been secretly dating when Noah discovered them together.

On the night of the accident, Lena had been driving away from an argument with Noah. He had followed her, desperate to stop her from leaving. Their cars had collided at the bridge.

Noah had told the police that he arrived afterward.

"He wasn't the stranger who saved me," Lena whispered.

"No," Adrian said. "He was the reason you needed saving."

Chapter 4: What Remained

Lena confronted Noah in the hospital garden.

"Did you lie to me?"

He broke down. He admitted following her, admitted the collision, and admitted hiding the truth because he feared losing her forever.

"I love you," he pleaded.

"Maybe you do. But love without honesty makes me afraid."

She asked him to leave while she healed. Adrian offered to stay, but Lena refused to let either man decide what her future should be.

Months later, she moved into her own apartment. Some memories returned; others never did. She learned to live without forcing herself to recover every missing piece.

One day, she found Noah's old photograph tucked inside a book. She looked at it without anger and placed it in a drawer.

She had lost part of her past. She would not lose her future trying to repair it.
