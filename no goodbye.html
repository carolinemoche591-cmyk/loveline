Some goodbyes arrive years too late.

No Goodbye

He waited five years for an answer. The letters explained everything except why she never returned.

Written by Loveline

Chapter 1: The Empty Room

The morning Mara disappeared, the kettle was still warm and two cups of tea sat untouched on the table.

Her boyfriend, Ethan, found a note beside his cup.

Please don't look for me. I'm sorry.

He called her friends, visited her workplace, and searched every place they had ever visited together. Nobody knew where she had gone.

For months, he waited for her to return. Eventually, he packed her belongings into a box but could not bring himself to throw anything away.

He never stopped wondering what he had done wrong.

Chapter 2: The Letters

Five years later, a package arrived at his door. Inside were twelve letters, all written in Mara's handwriting.

The first was dated the week she vanished.

Ethan, if you're reading this, I have failed to find the courage to tell you the truth.

Each letter revealed a piece of her secret. Mara had discovered that her father had stolen money from a business partner, and the partner was threatening her family. She had left to protect Ethan from being dragged into the danger.

But the final letter was different.

I know you will think I left because I stopped loving you. That is the lie I am most afraid you'll believe.

Chapter 3: The Photograph

The package contained an old photograph of Mara standing beside a hospital bed. The patient was a little girl with the same eyes.

Ethan contacted the return address and learned that Mara had spent years caring for her younger sister, who had been seriously ill. The family had moved to another country after the threats began.

He found a current address and wrote a letter of his own.

He did not demand an explanation. He simply told Mara that he had survived the silence, though it had changed him.

Three weeks later, she replied.

"I thought leaving was the only way to keep you safe," she wrote. "I didn't understand that silence could hurt you too."

Chapter 4: The Meeting

They met at the station where they had once planned to take their first trip together.

Mara looked older, more tired, but still familiar. Ethan wanted to run to her. Instead, he stayed where he was.

"I loved you," he said. "I would have faced the truth with you."

"I know that now."

"Why didn't you trust me?"

She had no answer that could repair five years.

They spoke until the sun began to set. Mara apologized without asking for another chance. Ethan listened without promising forgiveness.

When it was time to leave, she asked if he hated her.

"No," he said. "But I can't return to the person I was before you left."

They said goodbye properly this time.

Ethan went home and placed the letters in a drawer. The past would always be part of his story, but it no longer had to write the ending.
