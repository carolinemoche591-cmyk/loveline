THE LAST DRIVER

A Funny Thriller

CHAPTER ONE

The Car That Should Not Have Been There

Jamal Kato had three problems.

First, he was broke.

Second, he was late.

Third, he had absolutely no idea why a black Lamborghini was following him.

"Okay," Jamal said, looking into the side mirror.

The Lamborghini turned left.

Jamal turned left.

The Lamborghini turned right.

Jamal turned right.

The Lamborghini stopped.

Jamal stopped.

"Maybe we're friends," Jamal whispered.

The Lamborghini flashed its headlights.

"No. We are definitely not friends."

He was walking home because his motorcycle had broken down for the fourth time that month. He had been carrying a packet of chips in one hand and a half-dead phone in the other.

Then he saw the Lamborghini.

It was parked underneath a streetlight.

Beautiful.

Black.

Shiny.

Expensive.

The kind of car that made Jamal's wallet feel embarrassed just looking at it.

There was nobody inside.

Jamal walked past it.

Then he stopped.

He looked back.

A small envelope was sitting on the driver's seat.

He looked around.

Nobody.

He opened the door.

Inside the envelope was a key.

And one sentence.

DRIVE BEFORE MIDNIGHT.

Jamal laughed.

"Who writes things like this?"

His phone buzzed.

Unknown number.

He answered.

"Hello?"

Silence.

Then a man's voice whispered:

"Whatever you do..."

A pause.

"...don't let them find the car."

The call ended.

Jamal stared at the phone.

Then at the Lamborghini.

Then back at the phone.

"Absolutely not."

He closed the door.

He walked away.

Five steps later, he stopped.

He turned around.

"Wait."

He looked at the car again.

"Did he say Lamborghini?"

Jamal walked back.

"Just one drive."

He opened the door.

Sat down.

Put the key in.

The engine came alive.

VROOOOOOM.

Jamal's eyes widened.

"Oh..."

He smiled.

"Oh, this is irresponsible."

And that was how Jamal Kato accidentally entered the most dangerous street race in the country.

He just didn't know it yet.

CHAPTER TWO

The Midnight Race

At exactly 11:57 p.m., Jamal was driving through the city.

He had no idea where he was going.

The car did.

That was the problem.

The navigation system had turned itself on.

DESTINATION: UNKNOWN.

Jamal frowned.

"Unknown?"

The screen changed.

ARRIVAL: 12:00 A.M.

"Three minutes?"

He laughed nervously.

"Okay, car. You better know what you're doing."

The car accelerated.

Jamal grabbed the steering wheel.

"HEY!"

The speedometer climbed.

80.

100.

120.

"BRO!"

150.

"BROTHER!"

The car shot through an empty intersection.

Then another car appeared beside him.

A red Ferrari.

The driver looked at Jamal.

Jamal looked at him.

The Ferrari driver smiled.

Then pointed forward.

Jamal looked ahead.

A line of cars waited at a red light.

Lamborghinis.

Ferraris.

Porsches.

Muscle cars.

Even an old Toyota that looked like someone's grandmother had driven it there.

Jamal blinked.

"What is this?"

The traffic light turned green.

Every car launched forward.

Jamal's Lamborghini moved automatically.

"WAIT!"

The race had begun.

CHAPTER THREE

The Worst Driver in the Race

Jamal was terrible.

Absolutely terrible.

He missed the first corner.

He nearly hit a billboard.

He accidentally activated the windshield wipers.

Then the horn.

Then the headlights.

Then somehow the radio started playing wedding music.

"WHY IS THIS CAR LIKE THIS?"

The Ferrari passed him.

The Porsche passed him.

The old Toyota passed him.

Jamal stared.

"Even the grandmother car?"

The Toyota driver waved.

Jamal waved back.

Then the Toyota disappeared around a corner.

"This is humiliating."

Suddenly, three motorcycles appeared.

They surrounded him.

One rider shouted:

"GIVE US THE KEY!"

Jamal shouted back:

"WHAT KEY?"

"The key!"

Jamal looked down.

The ignition key was glowing blue.

"Oh."

The motorcycle riders accelerated.

Jamal panicked.

He slammed the accelerator.

The Lamborghini shot forward.

Jamal screamed.

The motorcycles followed.

Jamal screamed louder.

The radio suddenly changed.

A woman's voice spoke.

"Welcome, Jamal."

He froze.

"How do you know my name?"

"Because you were chosen."

"Chosen for what?"

The voice paused.

"To survive."

Jamal's smile disappeared.

Behind him, the motorcycles were getting closer.

And ahead...

The road suddenly ended.

CHAPTER FOUR

The Jump

Jamal saw the broken bridge.

"NO."

The road ended thirty metres ahead.

"NO, NO, NO!"

He slammed the brakes.

Nothing happened.

"BRAKES!"

The dashboard flashed.

BRAKES: LOL.

Jamal stared.

"You've got to be kidding me."

The Lamborghini reached the edge.

Jamal closed his eyes.

The car flew.

For three terrifying seconds, there was nothing beneath them.

Then—

BOOM!

The car landed on the other side.

Jamal opened one eye.

He was alive.

He looked at the dashboard.

The car displayed:

NICE LANDING.

Jamal breathed heavily.

"Thank you."

The car beeped.

YOU'RE WELCOME, IDIOT.

Jamal stared at the screen.

"Did my car just insult me?"

CHAPTER FIVE

The Man in the Suit

The race continued.

But now Jamal noticed something strange.

The other drivers weren't trying to win.

They were trying to stop him.

One car attempted to push him off the road.

Another fired a tracking device onto his bumper.

A third driver held up a sign.

GIVE US THE KEY.

Jamal shouted:

"WHY DOES EVERYONE WANT THIS STUPID KEY?"

The radio answered.

"Because it opens something."

"What?"

The radio crackled.

"The vault."

Jamal's stomach dropped.

"What vault?"

Before the voice could answer, a black SUV rammed his car from behind.

Jamal spun across the road.

He somehow avoided a crash.

The SUV pulled alongside him.

The passenger window lowered.

A man in a black suit looked at Jamal.

He held up a photograph.

It was Jamal.

Taken that morning.

Jamal's mouth dropped open.

The man shouted:

"You were never supposed to get the car!"

Jamal shouted back:

"I DIDN'T EVEN KNOW IT WAS YOUR CAR!"

The man pointed at him.

"Exactly!"

Then the SUV disappeared into the darkness.

Jamal stared.

"What does that even mean?"

The radio answered:

"It means you're in trouble."

Jamal sighed.

"I figured that out."

CHAPTER SIX

The Rules

The mysterious woman returned through the radio.

"Listen carefully."

Jamal gripped the steering wheel.

"I'm listening."

"There are six drivers left."

"Okay."

"Each driver has one part of the code."

"Okay."

"The first driver to reach the final checkpoint gets access to the vault."

Jamal frowned.

"And what is inside the vault?"

The voice went silent.

"Hello?"

Nothing.

"Are you there?"

Nothing.

Jamal sighed.

"Of course."

Then the voice returned.

"Jamal."

"Yeah?"

"If you win..."

A pause.

"...you'll become the richest man in the city."

Jamal smiled.

"Finally."

"But if you lose..."

Another pause.

"...they'll kill you."

Jamal's smile disappeared.

"Can we go back to the richest part?"

CHAPTER SEVEN

The Worst Alliance Ever

Jamal eventually discovered that the red Ferrari belonged to a woman named Zara.

She had been racing for years.

She was fast.

Smart.

Fearless.

And completely unimpressed by Jamal.

"You're terrible," she told him.

"I know."

"You don't know how to race."

"I know."

"You nearly crashed into a restaurant."

"I was hungry."

She stared at him.

"You're insane."

"Thank you."

"That wasn't a compliment."

"Oh."

They formed an alliance.

Unfortunately, Jamal immediately ruined it.

"What's your plan?"

Zara said, "We follow the mountain road."

Jamal nodded.

"Perfect."

He turned left.

Zara screamed:

"THAT'S THE OCEAN!"

Jamal slammed the brakes.

"Oh."

CHAPTER EIGHT

The Police

Police sirens appeared behind them.

Jamal looked at Zara.

"Did you call them?"

"No."

"Did you steal something?"

"No."

"Did you?"

"No!"

"Okay."

They looked at each other.

Then the police loudspeaker came alive.

"BLACK LAMBORGHINI, PULL OVER!"

Jamal sighed.

"Finally."

Zara stared at him.

"What?"

"Finally?"

"I've been waiting for someone to tell me what I'm doing wrong."

"Jamal, they're chasing us."

"Oh."

He accelerated.

The police followed.

Soon the race became a chase.

The chase became a disaster.

And the disaster became a national headline.

By morning, every television station was showing the same footage.

A black Lamborghini.

A red Ferrari.

Eight police cars.

And Jamal's face.

The headline read:

MYSTERY DRIVER TERRORIZES CITY.

Jamal looked at the television.

"That picture makes me look guilty."

Zara looked at him.

"You are guilty."

"Of what?"

"Driving like that."

CHAPTER NINE

The Vault

By sunrise, there were only three drivers left.

Jamal.

Zara.

And the man in the black SUV.

They reached an abandoned industrial building.

Inside was a giant metal door.

Jamal stepped out.

A screen appeared.

ENTER CODE.

Zara looked at Jamal.

"Where's the key?"

Jamal checked his pocket.

Gone.

He panicked.

"My key!"

The black SUV arrived.

The man stepped out.

He smiled.

"I took it."

Jamal stared at him.

"You stole my key?"

"Yes."

"From my pocket?"

"Yes."

"How?"

The man smiled.

"You talk too much."

Jamal looked offended.

"That is actually fair."

The man inserted the key.

The door opened.

Everyone went silent.

Inside was not money.

Not gold.

Not diamonds.

It was...

A collection of cars.

Hundreds of them.

Classic cars.

Supercars.

Racing cars.

Cars worth millions.

Zara stared.

Jamal whispered:

"I'm going to need a bigger parking lot."

Then the lights switched on.

At the back of the vault stood a single black car.

The same model as Jamal's.

But this one had a strange symbol on the hood.

The woman on the radio spoke one final time.

"That's the car."

Jamal looked at it.

"What does it do?"

The answer came quietly.

"It chooses its driver."

The black car suddenly started itself.

VROOOOOOM.

Jamal stepped backward.

The car's headlights turned toward him.

And then the doors unlocked.

CHAPTER TEN

The Real Driver

Jamal approached the car.

Zara grabbed his arm.

"Don't."

"Why?"

"We don't know what it is."

Jamal looked at the car.

Then at Zara.

Then at the hundreds of cars around them.

"After everything we've been through..."

He smiled.

"...you really think I'm going to walk away?"

Zara sighed.

"You're impossible."

Jamal opened the door.

The moment he sat inside, every screen in the vault switched on.

A countdown appeared.

10

Jamal swallowed.

9

Zara stepped closer.

8

The black SUV driver raised his weapon.

7

Police sirens sounded outside.

6

Jamal gripped the steering wheel.

5

The mysterious woman's voice whispered:

"Welcome to the real race."

4

Jamal smiled.

3

The engine roared.

2

The vault doors began closing.

1

The lights went out.

And the entire building exploded into darkness.

Then—

VROOOOOOOOOOM!

The black car disappeared through the vault wall.

Jamal screamed:

"WHY DOES THIS KEEP HAPPENING TO ME?!"

And somewhere in the darkness...

someone laughed.

THE RACE WAS ONLY BEGINNING.

THE END... FOR NOW.
